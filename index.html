Interactive introduction — Why eigenvalues?

A general vector may change both length and direction under a matrix transformation. An eigenvector is special: its direction is preserved, and only its scale or orientation changes.

Repeated transformations

If Av = λv, then Akv = λkv. The magnitude of λ predicts growth, decay or oscillation.

Long-term dynamics

In a system xk+1 = Axk, the eigenvalue with the largest magnitude usually controls the eventual behavior.

PDP

Fast matrix powers

A diagonal matrix is easy to raise to a power. Diagonalization changes coordinates to the eigenvector directions.

◎

Natural modes

Eigenvectors describe natural patterns in population models, networks, differential equations, vibrations and data analysis.

Triangular-matrix shortcut

The eigenvalues of an upper or lower triangular matrix are its diagonal entries. Change the entries and observe that the off-diagonal entries do not affect the characteristic roots.
